#### What is Gemini Enterprise app?

Gemini Enterprise app is an advanced agentic platform that brings the best of Google AI to every employee, for every workflow. It empowers teams to discover, create, share, and run AI agents—all in one secure environment.

#### What does Gemini Enterprise app do?

With Gemini Enterprise app, every individual in any organization can shift from tedious tasks to high-impact work, thereby enhancing team productivity, fostering creativity, and improving business outcomes.

With Gemini Enterprise app, your employees can access Google’s powerful AI models through an intuitive chat interface, deploy ready-to-use agents made by Google and our thriving ecosystem of partners, and even create their own custom agents with a powerful no-code workbench, all while maintaining your security, privacy and governance over all the agents.

#### Who should use Gemini Enterprise app?

Gemini Enterprise app is designed to benefit all teams across diverse business sectors and organizations. It empowers knowledge workers in departments like sales, marketing, finance, HR, and IT/engineering, as well as frontline staff such as warehouse managers and retail associates, to gain insights faster and automate routine tasks.

#### What are the differences between Gemini Enterprise app editions?

Gemini Enterprise app offers multiple editions to fit the unique needs of your organization.

**Business edition**: Great for small businesses and teams up to 300—no IT setup needed.

**Standard and Plus editions**: Designed for organizations that need stricter security and compliance requirements. Includes higher quota, unlimited seats, and access to third-party agents.

**Pay-as-you-go edition:** Lets you run agent workloads without hitting quota limits mid-task. There is no upfront commitment or base subscription fee. You pay only for the compute and tokens your teams consume at standard model API rates.

**Frontline edition**: Catered to frontline workers. Standard and Plus edition customers have the option to purchase the Frontline edition as an add-on.

If you are a government or educational institution, we provide tailored offerings like Gemini for Government to address your specific needs. Contact your Google Cloud sales representative to learn more.

#### How do I access Gemini Enterprise app?

Here are two ways you can get started today:

- [Try Business edition free for 30 days](https://business.gemini.google/?facet_url=%2Fgemini-enterprise): Great for individuals, small businesses, and teams up to 300—no IT setup needed.
- [Try Standard or Plus edition free for 30 days](https://console.cloud.google.com/freetrial?redirectPath=gemini-enterprise): Get advanced features, including higher quota, unlimited seats, enterprise-grade compliance, and access to third party agents.

To learn more about purchasing the Standard, Plus or Frontline editions, please [contact Google Cloud sales](https://cloud.google.com/contact/gemini-enterprise).

#### Who owns the data and does Google use my data for training models?

The Business, Standard and Plus editions of Gemini Enterprise app are built on Google’s secure-by-design infrastructure, giving you the peace of mind to deploy AI agents across your organization confidently.

- You own your data, not Google
- Your data—including prompts, outputs, and training—isn’t used to train Google models or models for any other customer
- Google never sells customer data to third parties
- Google doesn’t use customer data for advertising

#### How do Gemini Enterprise app–Standard and Plus editions work with developer tools, like Google Antigravity and Android Studio?

Currently rolling out to select customers, Standard and Plus edition users can access:

- Google Antigravity: A suite of agentic developer tools that allow developers to offload coding tasks to AI agents running in parallel.
- Gemini in Android Studio: Agentic Android development tooling that helps your team build high-quality apps faster.

As part of Gemini Enterprise app, admins can monitor usage, set spending thresholds, and enforce security guardrails, giving teams everything they need to build safely and at scale.
